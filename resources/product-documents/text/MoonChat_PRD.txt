MoonChat product requirements
OPC OS portfolio  |  30 September 2026  |  Version 1 for product review
MoonChat should remain a portable review contract for artifact-producing applications. Its frozen baseline binds an artifact revision to questions, objections, proposals, participants and append-only decisions; it does not operate live chat or accept company knowledge on another product’s behalf. Five JavaScript review tests passed in the portfolio investigation. That bounded core evidence does not qualify a host review screen, an end-to-end founder decision or a completed MoonFind to MoonBook handoff. The next release decision is whether one real host can make review understandable and return a digest-bound receipt without obscuring dissent.
Repository maturity claim native v1 capability; L1 structured, consumer UI wiring product-owned
This document defines the product baseline and a separately proposed roadmap. Acceptance tests below are release criteria unless a cited observation explicitly says they passed. Current source coverage does not establish production deployment or customer value.
Product philosophy and scope
Accountability requires knowing what was reviewed, who decided and which uncertainty remains. A conversational transcript is useful evidence, but the volume or fluency of its turns cannot establish approval. MoonChat should make the distinction between a question, an objection, a proposal and an authorized decision machine-readable while retaining the original source binding. A later artifact revision must require an explicit new review scope rather than inheriting the approval of older bytes.
The primary customer is a pack or host developer. A founder encounters MoonChat through the artifact they are already considering, not through another destination or inbox. In the operating sequence from source to understanding, commitment, execution and inspected artifact, MoonChat supports the review step. MoonProj owns commitments, MoonClaw owns live conversations and execution, and MoonBook independently owns knowledge acceptance. Customer acceptance and cash receipt are outside this review contract.

User scenarios
Create a portable review bundle
MOONCHAT-W01 | Baseline scope. A producing pack has an artifact requiring review
1. Capture the artifact identifier, revision and digest
2. Add participants and required decision roles
3. Attach questions, objections and proposals to that scope
4. Export the versioned bundle and display its projected review state
Edge case An artifact changes after review starts; the host must distinguish the old binding from the new revision.
Failure and recovery Missing source identity or an unsupported schema produces a validation error and no authoritative review projection.
Acceptance Import a valid bundle in a second fixture consumer; reject a changed digest and an incomplete required identity without silently substituting current bytes.
Evidence references docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Ownership; docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Canonical identities
Retain and replay the review journal
MOONCHAT-W02 | Baseline scope. A reviewer adds a decision or changes an earlier position
1. Write a new content-addressed event
2. Retain prior rejected or deferred evidence
3. Replay the ordered journal into a current projection
4. Export the evidence needed to explain that projection
Edge case A repeated submission has the same event content; it must not create a second logical decision.
Failure and recovery Truncated, inconsistent or unsupported journal content is surfaced as incomplete evidence; it cannot be repaired by overwriting earlier events.
Acceptance Replay the same journal twice and compare projected state; append a superseding decision and confirm that prior dissent remains available.
Evidence references docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Import, export and versioning; moon-luna-investigation-ledger-20260930.md
Produce a MoonFlow review record
MOONCHAT-W03 | Baseline scope. MoonFlow requests a conversation review record
1. Validate the operation identity and input bundle
2. Evaluate the required named human decisions
3. Emit the record with its supported claim ceiling
4. Let MoonFlow reconcile the operation result without expanding its authority
Edge case An incomplete or deferred decision set must not yield accepted knowledge merely because the adapter succeeded.
Failure and recovery Unknown decision authority or source mismatch returns a nonqualifying record or structured refusal.
Acceptance Exercise complete, missing, deferred and mismatched approvals; only the complete authorized case may qualify for the documented accepted knowledge ceiling.
Evidence references docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Capability status; docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Canonical identities
Consume review evidence in a product
MOONCHAT-W04 | Baseline scope. MoonFind exports stakeholder review or MoonBook receives a closure candidate
1. The producer exports evidence with source binding
2. The receiver validates identity and supported version
3. The receiver shows review state alongside its own domain state
4. The receiver independently chooses import and acceptance
Edge case A receiver may preserve a review without accepting its claims. A newer artifact may coexist with the reviewed revision.
Failure and recovery Missing receiving integration leaves the handoff pending; exporting a bundle alone is not a completed closure.
Acceptance A fixture retains rejected evidence and independently rejects knowledge intake even when a review record contains approval.
Evidence references docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Import, export and versioning
Review a founder objection in context
MOONCHAT-W05 | Roadmap scope. A MoonProj commitment or MoonFind artifact contains a consequential unresolved objection
1. Open review from the producing record
2. Show the exact source, proposed action, named decider and unresolved objections
3. Record a human choice with reasons
4. Return the receipt to the source record
5. Confirm the host shows the decision without claiming execution or customer acceptance
Edge case A reviewer switches artifact revisions mid-session; the host must restart or explicitly rebind review.
Failure and recovery A disconnected return path retains the decision locally as pending delivery and supports idempotent retry.
Acceptance A named tester completes approve, reject and defer journeys in one host; receipt readback matches displayed artifact and retained dissent after reload.
Evidence references docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Import, export and versioning

Alternatives and optimality
Structured review fields in one host
Advantage Lowest implementation cost for one simple approval flow
Cost No portable replay contract until a second consumer exists
When to choose Prefer when no cross product evidence exchange is needed
Comments in an artifact editor
Advantage Immediate context with familiar interaction
Cost Comments alone may lack source digest and named decision semantics
When to choose Use for informal discussion; add MoonChat only at a consequential review boundary
Standalone review application
Advantage Central queue and dedicated review controls
Cost Adds navigation and separates the decision from its producing context
When to choose Reject for this scope; embed in existing hosts
MoonChat is most useful as a small review contract with a host acceptance obligation. A separate chat application would duplicate MoonClaw and MoonDesk without proving that a human understood the exact artifact. Portability matters only when another product can interpret the receipt’s authority.
Baseline readiness should depend on identity, replay and claim semantics. The five passing tests are bounded evidence. Keep consumer gaps explicit and require receipt readback before claiming an end-to-end decision journey.
A host-native decision field wins for one simple reviewer with no portability need. MoonChat earns its structure when revisions, retained dissent, multiple decisions or independent receivers make informal comments insufficient.
Qualify one artifact type, one producer and one receiving record first. This narrow slice tests the difficult authority and delivery boundaries. Richer conversations and review dashboards should wait until it demonstrates understandable decisions and recoverable receipt linkage.
