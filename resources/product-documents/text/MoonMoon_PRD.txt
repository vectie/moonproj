MoonMoon product requirements
OPC OS portfolio  |  30 September 2026  |  Version 1 for product review
MoonMoon is a lunar terrain and mission digital simulator for specialists preparing an inspectable mission recommendation. Its baseline covers data-root cataloging, terrain and route analysis, typed robot-mission simulation and read-only 3D/CLI inspection. Native 208/208 and the supported read-only browser path passed locally. The inspected mission remains blocked by negative energy, and the optional E1 archive is unavailable. No physical mission readiness or external dataset truth was established.
Repository maturity claim digital simulation alpha
This document defines the product baseline and a separately proposed roadmap. Acceptance tests below are release criteria unless a cited observation explicitly says they passed. Current source coverage does not establish production deployment or customer value.
Product philosophy and scope
A lunar scene is a view of declared inputs, not proof of the terrain. A useful mission recommendation binds coverage, coordinate frame, units, digests and uncertainty to hazard, route and energy calculations. Missing coverage should remain visible because a smooth render or a plausible route can otherwise imply confidence that the source data does not support.
This pack serves a narrow lunar or robotics offer rather than a general founder workspace. An accountable operator approves a digital study, inspects the resulting mission dossier and records a reviewed recommendation. MoonRobo must independently evaluate any later physical test using current hardware and safety evidence. Digital completion, customer acceptance and cash receipt are separate facts, and none grants physical authority.

User scenarios
Lunar data root and first site catalog
MN-W01 | Baseline scope. An analyst registers a site for digital study
1. Select the declared data root and supported source
2. Record frame, units, coverage, digest and uncertainty
3. Create the first-site catalog entry
4. Expose available fixture and optional archive capabilities separately
Edge case An optional archive may be absent while supported read-only fixture paths remain usable.
Failure and recovery Unknown frame, incompatible units or inaccessible source prevents qualified analysis rather than triggering an invented substitute.
Acceptance The catalog shows which data is present and why an unavailable archive blocks only the dependent capability.
Evidence references README.mbt.md#Shape; docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Evidence and claims
Terrain hazards route and energy assessment
MN-W02 | Baseline scope. A designer proposes a mission corridor
1. Compute slope, roughness and declared hazard measures
2. Assess route, horizon and energy using explicit assumptions
3. Identify missing coverage and remediation choices
4. Inspect the resulting digital feasibility decision
Edge case A route may be geometrically plausible but energetically infeasible; negative energy must remain a blocker.
Failure and recovery An invalid or incomplete corridor stops affected calculations and preserves diagnostic inputs.
Acceptance A reviewer can trace each decision-driving constraint to data and assumptions; the current blocked mission is never labeled physically ready.
Evidence references docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Capability status; README.mbt.md#Run
Typed robot mission simulation
MN-W03 | Baseline scope. A validated digital robot design is supplied
1. Read the moonsuite.robot-design.v1 payload
2. Bind profile, URDF and validation identities from MoonRobo
3. Validate relative artifact paths and simulation-only authority
4. Run the supported digital mission and retain exact receipts
Edge case Structural robot design validation is not mission suitability or hardware readiness; each remains a distinct claim.
Failure and recovery Mismatched design identities, unsupported paths or expanded authority are rejected before simulation.
Acceptance The receipt names the exact design and mission revision and carries no physical dispatch grant.
Evidence references README.mbt.md#Bound robot mission simulation; docs/CAPABILITY_PACK.md
Read only 3D and CLI inspection
MN-W04 | Baseline scope. An operator opens the terrain guide or mission result
1. Use the supported Rabbita 3D or CLI inspection route
2. Read source and mission status alongside the visualization
3. Inspect the negative-energy blocker or missing optional E1 archive
4. Keep read-only navigation available for supported inputs
Edge case A visual model can load even when a mission cannot run; those states must not collapse into a generic ready badge.
Failure and recovery Missing archive reports bridge unavailable; the application does not fabricate or download an unapproved archive.
Acceptance The latest local guide path remains readable, the mission blocker is visible and no device action occurs.
Evidence references README.mbt.md#Run; docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Capability status
Mission alternative review
MN-W05 | Roadmap scope. A designer needs to choose between proposed digital routes
1. Freeze common site and robot input identities
2. Compare route, energy, hazard and missing coverage
3. Present sensitivity to decision-driving assumptions
4. Record a named approval of only the digital recommendation
Edge case A safer route with poor coverage may remain incomparable rather than receive a false composite ranking.
Failure and recovery Changed source data invalidates affected comparisons and requires a new reviewed revision.
Acceptance The operator can explain why one alternative was selected or why none is supported.
Evidence references docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Evidence and claims; docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Capability status
Simulation to robot evidence bridge
MN-W06 | Roadmap scope. A robotics operator requests evidence for a separate trial
1. Export the exact versioned mission and simulation receipts
2. Deliver through the declared MoonFlow adapter contract
3. Have MoonRobo verify identity and current hardware evidence independently
4. Record refusal or separate physical authorization without changing simulation history
Edge case A successful digital simulation can still lead to refusal because calibration or safety evidence is stale.
Failure and recovery An incompatible receipt or missing handoff acknowledgement leaves the bridge pending and prevents implied authorization.
Acceptance The receiving system can cite the simulation but cannot derive physical permission from it.
Evidence references README.mbt.md#Bound robot mission simulation; docs/CAPABILITY_PACK.md

MoonMoon should optimize for one defensible digital mission decision. The visual environment helps people inspect a corridor, but its value depends on keeping evidence and uncertainty close enough that a rendered surface cannot be mistaken for validated terrain.
The negative-energy blocker and unavailable optional archive are honest product states. Hiding them to create a smoother demo would undermine the purpose of the tool. Native 208/208 and read-only browser success support bounded local maturity without proving external source quality, deployment or a live robotics loop.
A narrower first release could offer one site, one robot design contract and one repeatable mission dossier with manual review. Multi-site expansion, new robot classes and broad autonomy should wait until restart, replay and source qualification are dependable.
A GIS tool or established simulator is preferable when it already answers the specialist question with less integration. MoonMoon earns its place when source-bound terrain, mission constraints and robot evidence need a shared, reviewable identity. That tradeoff is conditional and cannot establish universal optimality.
