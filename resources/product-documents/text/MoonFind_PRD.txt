MoonFind product requirements
OPC OS portfolio  |  30 September 2026  |  Version 1 for product review
MoonFind should deliver an inspectable answer to a bounded decision question, with openable files, source provenance, contradictions and unresolved verification gaps. Its local startup and missing-gateway behavior have been tested. A configured provider-backed research delivery remains a qualification gate.
Repository maturity claim evidence-backed research alpha
This document defines the product baseline and a separately proposed roadmap. Acceptance tests below are release criteria unless a cited observation explicitly says they passed. Current source coverage does not establish production deployment or customer value.
Product philosophy and scope
A researcher buys a usable answer and evidence, not an impressive agent graph. Every job begins with audience, decision, source limits, desired files and acceptance criteria. The researcher must be able to reject an answer, inspect the source and revise the brief while preserving what was previously delivered.
Research can reduce uncertainty but cannot establish that a customer will buy. For the OPC spine, MoonFind explains what is known and what would falsify a belief. MoonProj retains the decision and commitment; MoonBook retains reviewed learning. Specialized robotics or lunar series remain optional templates behind this general file-delivery contract.
Users and jobs
researcher
Convert a bounded research question into an answer and openable source-backed files.
Trigger A decision depends on evidence the researcher has not yet assembled.
Successful outcome The delivered files answer the brief and expose contradictions and missing verification.
founder deciding what to test
Choose what business assumption to test next using research and a falsifying customer experiment.
Trigger A buyer, offer or strategy decision has competing explanations.
Successful outcome The founder understands the evidence and uncertainty without treating research as demand proof.
domain expert reviewer
Evaluate technical adequacy and material claims in the delivered artifact.
Trigger A manifest-complete research package is submitted for domain review.
Successful outcome The expert can accept, reject or narrow conclusions with a digest-bound review record.
operator configuring MoonClaw
Configure the gateway and route with the required runtime authority.
Trigger A research brief is ready but execution is unavailable or unhealthy.
Successful outcome The route is genuinely usable or the missing prerequisite stays explicit; no readiness is guessed.

User scenarios
General brief for literature  local corpus  comparison  evidence brief or experiment plan
MOONFIND-UC01 | Baseline scope. Turn an uncertain question into a bounded research brief.
1. Choose literature review, local corpus, comparison, evidence brief or experiment-plan intent.
2. Specify objective, audience, source scope, output filenames and criteria.
3. Review the brief before any runtime submission and retain it with the job identity.
Edge case A broad question with no decision or output criterion needs narrowing rather than immediate execution.
Failure and recovery An incomplete brief remains editable and cannot appear as a completed research answer.
Acceptance For each template, another reviewer can identify the decision and evaluate the promised files without inspecting agent internals.
Evidence references docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Outcome; README.mbt.md#Start a general research job
Source intake and provenance
MOONFIND-UC02 | Baseline scope. Ingest evidence within source rights and provenance limits.
1. Add pasted text, CSV, JSON, BibTeX, a local file or allowed public HTTPS/PDF snapshot.
2. Preserve origin, retrieval record, content digest and applicable source restrictions.
3. Distinguish full text, metadata and abstract availability in the evidence view.
Edge case arXiv metadata and abstracts must not be presented as a licensed full-text review.
Failure and recovery Denied licensed access or unavailable snapshots yield explicit gaps; no fallback should silently change the authorized source scope.
Acceptance A changed source produces a new snapshot identity; denied input cannot enter the evidence set as if read.
Evidence references README.mbt.md#Start a general research job; docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Evidence and document security
MoonClaw plan run  pause resume and child revision
MOONFIND-UC03 | Baseline scope. Run a job through the configured MoonClaw gateway.
1. Select a valid model route explicitly after runtime readiness is known.
2. Submit the reviewed plan to MoonClaw and retain session, proposal and run identity.
3. Pause, resume the same job or create a child revision that preserves earlier outputs.
Edge case A missing gateway or blank route must not show Ready or allow a misleading job start.
Failure and recovery After interruption recover the same run before starting another; uncertain provider outcome stays unresolved.
Acceptance The blocked local path remains truthful; qualify a separate real configured run before marking this end-to-end path fulfilled.
Evidence references README.mbt.md#Start a general research job; docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Capability status
Files  preview  complete ZIP and delivery manifest
MOONFIND-UC04 | Baseline scope. Inspect and deliver a complete research file set.
1. Open each requested file and inspect its actual bytes.
2. Compare the manifest against filenames, SHA-256 digests and submitted criteria receipts.
3. Download the complete ZIP and leave missing or stale artifacts visible.
Edge case A valid digest proves byte identity, not scientific truth or satisfaction of a subjective criterion.
Failure and recovery A missing file or changed digest blocks complete delivery status and allows a revision without overwriting the previous manifest.
Acceptance An intentionally removed file and a modified file are both detected; a reviewer can open all files from the final manifest.
Evidence references README.mbt.md#Start a general research job; docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Outcome
Humanoid arXiv specialization and MoonTown challenge
MOONFIND-UC05 | Baseline scope. Challenge a specialized humanoid or arXiv hypothesis.
1. Select the specialized research question and permitted paper sources.
2. Develop counter-hypotheses and a smallest falsifying experiment alongside the synthesis.
3. Send a scoped challenge to MoonTown when the required participating services exist.
Edge case A fixture-only robotics graph is not an installed catalog of runnable capabilities.
Failure and recovery Unavailable specialization nodes are identified before execution and the user can continue a simpler file-based brief.
Acceptance The report distinguishes evidence, interpretation and experimental prediction; every executable claim cites installed capability evidence.

Lunar Habitat Decision Series
MOONFIND-UC06 | Baseline scope. Run a staged Lunar Habitat Decision Series.
1. Create a series with decision stages and named human review points.
2. Deliver the file set and uncertainty register for the current stage.
3. Advance only after the stage decision is recorded, carrying unresolved assumptions forward.
Edge case Later-stage artifacts cannot retroactively make an earlier human gate pass.
Failure and recovery Rejected findings create a revision of the stage and retain its prior sources and decision history.
Acceptance A series replay demonstrates a blocked human gate, a revision and explicit stage advancement.

Decision linked research brief
MOONFIND-F01 | Roadmap scope. Proposed extension after the owning baseline workflow and authority gates are qualified.
1. Receive a commitment ID, question, audience, deadline and source constraints from MoonProj.
2. Validate the payload and let the researcher refine the brief without changing company authority.

Acceptance Round-trip the commitment link without exposing secrets or creating a company decision.

Delivery roadmap
Phase 1 Qualify the baseline
Reproduce the product's critical local, contract and configured paths with exact versions and observable receipts. Close missing prerequisites without widening authority.
Requirements MOONFIND-B01, MOONFIND-B02, MOONFIND-B03, MOONFIND-B04, MOONFIND-B05, MOONFIND-B06
Exit gate Every P0 baseline acceptance has an explicit result and evidence locator; unresolved dependency and integration states remain visible.
Phase 2 Complete one connected outcome
Implement the smallest useful cross-product scenario and test review, interruption and recovery with real authorized inputs.
Requirements MOONFIND-R01, MOONFIND-R02
Exit gate A named user accepts the exact output and can recover its evidence chain without reconstructing identities manually.
Phase 3 Expand only after measured value
Add remaining proposed workflows where observed use demonstrates reduced coordination cost or a distinct required capability.
Requirements MOONFIND-R03
Exit gate Measure the outcome against the simpler alternative, include review overhead, and retain the ability to stop or narrow low-value scope.

Non goals
Direct provider invocation outside MoonClaw
Automatically validating scientific truth through file hashes
Treating user-supplied offline thesis findings as a new provenance-audit task
Proving demand from literature, agent completion or a research template
Alternatives and optimality
Manual literature search and an editable memo
Advantage Low setup burden for a one-off narrow question
Cost Source snapshots and revision lineage are manually maintained
When to choose Use when a short search solves the decision more quickly than configuring a gateway
A graph first specialist research environment
Advantage Expresses complex technical dependencies for expert users
Cost Large stage inventories can obscure answer quality and installed readiness
When to choose Use only after the generic file outcome is reliable and the specialization needs the graph
The strongest PRD keeps a general file-delivery surface and makes decision relevance observable. The specialized graphs are useful only where they change the quality of the answer. Promoting them into default navigation would increase founder coordination without demonstrated benefit.
The baseline evidence currently supports honest startup and blocked-state handling, not a completed provider research job. The next qualification should take one real permitted brief through gateway execution, file inspection, revision and acceptance.
The proposed decision-linked handoff should carry minimal context and a stable identity. It should not copy company authority into a research tool or let citation-rich prose masquerade as a confirmed customer need.
