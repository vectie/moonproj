MoonCast product requirements
OPC OS portfolio  |  30 September 2026  |  Version 1 for product review
MoonCast is a rights-aware media production studio for a small producer taking a qualified lead through quote, bounded production, reviewed master and client delivery. The frozen baseline includes commercial intake, creative and rights gates, the G0–G7 production chain, graph/editor/review tools and local client-review surfaces. The corrected local host truthfully shows an empty portfolio and blocks invalid zero-baseline leads. Real-provider production, paid design-partner episodes, external client acceptance and payment remain unverified.
Repository maturity claim deterministic pipeline alpha
This document defines the product baseline and a separately proposed roadmap. Acceptance tests below are release criteria unless a cited observation explicitly says they passed. Current source coverage does not establish production deployment or customer value.
Product philosophy and scope
A studio creates value when a client can inspect and accept a promised media deliverable. Graph nodes, completed provider jobs and an internal on-track indicator are intermediate evidence. The product should preserve one commercial commitment from sourced needs through approved production and an inspected artifact, then record client acceptance and cash receipt as independent facts.
Rights and cost authority must precede generation rather than be reconstructed after a master exists. A named producer owns scope and editorial quality; a commercial approver owns the quote; the client owns acceptance. Provider cost, labor, revenue and founder hours remain unknown when evidence is absent. This lets the studio learn from an actual episode without turning fixture output or forecast margin into business performance.

User scenarios
Qualified lead quote capacity and project intake
MC-W01 | Baseline scope. A producer enters a potential three to eight minute episode
1. Record required lead fields and positive observed schedule baseline
2. Model finite capacity, margin assumptions and founder hours
3. Freeze an immutable quote with named commercial, production and client decisions
4. Convert the exact approved quote to a project draft
Edge case An empty portfolio has no schedule result; zero baseline is invalid and unknown commercial inputs are not free resources.
Failure and recovery Missing lead evidence prevents recording; changed scope requires a new quote rather than silently altering the approved one.
Acceptance The local lead gate rejects baseline zero; the complete quote-to-project acceptance still requires qualification.

Alternatives and optimality
Standard editor and shared review folder
Advantage Familiar and fast for a simple single episode
Cost Manual rights and cost reconciliation
When to choose Prefer when the producer can maintain a reliable small ledger without orchestrated generation
Existing studio project management tools
Advantage Established client and scheduling workflows
Cost May not bind AI attempts and asset lineage deeply

Advantage Flexible experiments and advanced controls
Cost High setup and coordination cost for the first client job

MoonCast has a clear specialist company journey: a feasible paid promise can become a reviewed media artifact. The PRD is strongest when one quote identity survives that entire journey. Adding more creative controls before this continuity works risks producing impressive demos with weak commercial evidence.
The local empty-state and lead-gate fixes matter because false on-track status can influence a real commitment. Their verification is narrow and valuable. Native 75/75 and UI 5/5 do not prove rights-cleared provider use, deployed identity, client access or paid demand.
A narrower initial release could center on one episode format, one provider configuration and a manually reconciled commercial ledger. An ordinary editor and review folder remain the better choice for low-volume work if the producer can maintain rights and delivery evidence reliably.
Expansion should be justified by observed review burden, lost lineage, cost surprises or repeated client needs. Two paid design-partner episodes are a useful qualification gate, not proof of universal product-market fit or optimality. The product must preserve the ability to say that an episode was delivered but not yet accepted or paid.
