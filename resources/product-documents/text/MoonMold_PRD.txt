MoonMold product requirements
OPC OS portfolio  |  30 September 2026  |  Version 1 for product review
MoonMold is a bounded digital spatial-modeling pack for turning constrained intent into editable source and derived representations with validation and lineage. Its baseline is a typed semantic API, a qualified mock digital procedure, operation and recovery contracts, and an operator review surface. A synthetic validate, build and reload journey now passes locally. Live Blender qualification, broad parametric CAD and physical readiness remain outside the verified capability.
Repository maturity claim early digital-model alpha
This document defines the product baseline and a separately proposed roadmap. Acceptance tests below are release criteria unless a cited observation explicitly says they passed. Current source coverage does not establish production deployment or customer value.
Product philosophy and scope
A visually convincing model does not establish dimensional accuracy, engineering fitness or manufacturing safety. The editable source and its frame, units, digest and constraints are the reference. Presentation, simulation and manufacturing-candidate siblings must declare their relationships and known losses so a client can understand what each representation supports.
For a digital-modeling founder, the accountable loop begins with a sourced brief and a bounded promise: deliver a specific inspectable digital artifact. Approved execution produces receipts and files; a named reviewer evaluates them; the client separately accepts the agreed package. Payment and any later fabrication require their own evidence and authority. The pack should make this narrow loop reliable before adding a general-purpose modeling studio.

User scenarios
Semantic request and representation lineage
MM-W01 | Baseline scope. A designer requests a bounded spatial artifact
1. Select a supported typed operation
2. Declare dimensions, units, coordinate frame and source digest
3. Create editable source and requested derived siblings
4. Record representation purpose, parent identity and known losses
Edge case A manufacturing-candidate representation remains analysis data; its existence grants no machine or printer authority.
Failure and recovery Unsupported arbitrary script or shell content is rejected; ambiguous units require correction rather than a guessed conversion.
Acceptance The reviewer can traverse every sibling to exact source and identify dimensional or semantic losses.

Alternatives and optimality
Blender and a manual delivery checklist
Advantage Direct creative control and mature modeling tools
Cost Manual lineage and recovery evidence
When to choose Prefer for one experienced designer when reproducibility and automation add little value
Established parametric CAD
Advantage Mature assemblies, dimensions and engineering workflows
Cost Heavier setup and different licensing or collaboration model
When to choose Prefer whenever actual CAD constraint or certification requirements dominate
Bounded MoonMold pack
Advantage Repeatable semantic operations and explicit representation truth
Cost Requires adapter qualification and host integration
When to choose Choose for repeated constrained digital deliverables with meaningful provenance needs
MoonMold is most credible when it delivers one digital artifact that another person can inspect and reuse. The semantic API and representation-loss model are useful constraints: they prevent an attractive preview from becoming an unsupported engineering claim and reduce the execution surface that must be qualified.
The fixed null recovery, command-argument linkage and operation-parent path now make the mock journey coherent. That is meaningful local progress, but it says little about Blender interruption behavior or the engineering adequacy of a client model. Those require specific environment and review evidence.
A narrower first offer should select one repeatable object family or spatial transformation, one unit/frame convention and a clear source-plus-report package. Broad parametric CAD and fabrication promises would make the product harder to validate while competing with mature tools.
The right choice depends on repetition and evidence burden. For an occasional bespoke model, Blender plus a checklist can be simpler and better. MoonMold earns its integration cost only if exact lineage, controlled execution and recovery reduce observed rework without obscuring designer judgment.
