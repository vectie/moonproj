MoonBook product requirements
OPC OS portfolio  |  30 September 2026  |  Version 1 for product review
MoonBook should preserve company memory as raw sources, editable knowledge, reviewed outcomes and reproducible projections. Local ingest and timestamp-safe state exchange have bounded evidence. The highest-priority product decision is separating imported-source integrity from human factual review; the current accepted and verified labels must not imply that review occurred.
Repository maturity claim local alpha; contract claims L2 local Bookkeeper journey
This document defines the product baseline and a separately proposed roadmap. Acceptance tests below are release criteria unless a cited observation explicitly says they passed. Current source coverage does not establish production deployment or customer value.
Product philosophy and scope
Durable memory must make corrections possible. Raw source bytes, accepted knowledge and generated views have different owners and meanings. A founder should be able to see why a claim exists, what contradicted it, who reviewed it and what later outcome changed the interpretation.
Executable books can declare intent and propose work, but accepted book truth and operational authority remain separate. Bookkeeper learning should compare intention, execution and observed outcome before a person accepts a reusable rule. The purpose is less repeated reconstruction and fewer repeated mistakes, not automatic self-improving authority.

Successful outcome The user finds applicable reviewed guidance and its evidence without reconstructing the history.
book curator reviewer
Separate imported material from reviewed claims and approve corrections within their scope.
Trigger A source, synthesized claim or Bookkeeper outcome reaches a review queue.
Successful outcome Accepted knowledge has a clear reviewer, basis, version and correction path.
operator
Maintain book health, projections, backups and portable pack compatibility.
Trigger A book is installed, exported, upgraded or recovered after failure.
Successful outcome Raw evidence and accepted history survive a reproducible restore or rollback.
MoonClaw MoonFlow consumer
Consume exact versioned declarations and return artifacts for book-owned review.

Successful outcome The runtime can act on the declaration without interpreting it as self-approved truth or permissions.

User scenarios
Book lifecycle and static book rendering
MOONBOOK-UC01 | Baseline scope. Create and maintain a readable book.
1. Initialize a book and its structure using the documented book and wiki CLI.
2. Use SUMMARY-driven rendering, serving or watch to create reader projections.
3. Run doctor and inspect pack or skill structure before publishing a reusable local bundle.
Edge case A generated view is replaceable output and must not become a competing authoritative source.
Failure and recovery If rendering fails, preserve editable book content and report the broken section or input rather than overwriting source files.
Acceptance A clean book can be rendered and rebuilt from its source; missing entries are reported with their locations.
Evidence references README.mbt.md#Current Capabilities; docs/FEATURE_MATRIX.md#CLI
Raw first wiki ingest  query  review and lint
MOONBOOK-UC02 | Baseline scope. Ingest a source and query its retained context.
1. Store the raw input before deriving a wiki page.
2. Ingest a founder work packet and retain source, problem and acceptance text exactly.
3. Query with citations and inspect review and lint surfaces for subsequent curation.
Edge case Current ingest can label an unreviewed page accepted and verified; these labels must be interpreted as existing mechanics, not factual validation.
Failure and recovery On malformed input preserve the raw source and surface a repairable ingest failure; repeated ingest must not silently overwrite accepted claims.
Acceptance Round-trip the synthetic packet text; distinguish import integrity, claim review and query interpretation in the qualification record.
Evidence references docs/WIKI_WORKFLOW.md#Ingest Sources; docs/WIKI_WORKFLOW.md#Review; docs/WIKI_WORKFLOW.md#Lint
Source  knowledge bundle  graph and state projections
MOONBOOK-UC03 | Baseline scope. Export portable state and knowledge projections.
1. Generate state, bundle and graph outputs from the book.
2. Serialize timestamps as exact numeric epoch milliseconds within the documented safe range.
3. Decode the resulting current state through the MoonTown adapter and retain source references.
Edge case Legacy overflowed negative or small values can remain historical corrupted times; decoding does not repair chronology.
Failure and recovery Reject noninteger, nonfinite or unsafe numeric timestamps at the consumer boundary and retain the original snapshot for diagnosis.
Acceptance Test realistic millisecond values through producer and consumer; source references and field types remain unchanged.
Evidence references docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Capability status; README.mbt.md#Quick Start
Executable work declaration and review queue
MOONBOOK-UC04 | Baseline scope. Declare executable work without promoting its output.
1. Prepare a versioned book intent and requirements artifact.
2. Import the exact declaration into MoonFlow after required review and authority checks.
3. Return produced artifacts to a review queue before updating accepted knowledge.
Edge case A successful work attempt is not an accepted book claim or a new permission.
Failure and recovery Incompatible import versions or stale evidence block activation and preserve the declaration for correction.
Acceptance wiki.requirements.prepare@0.1.0 produces a review-pending artifact whose execution cannot directly rewrite accepted evidence.
Evidence references docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Ownership; docs/EXECUTABLE_BOOK_BOUNDARY.md
Keeper standing watch and Bookkeeper Three Gap outcome records
MOONBOOK-UC05 | Baseline scope. Close an outcome with Bookkeeper and Keeper.
1. Maintain an explicit standing watch or intent with acceptance conditions.
2. Submit intent, execution and post-delivery observations as a Three-Gap record.
3. Have a named reviewer accept, reject or narrow the proposed correction before capability promotion.
Edge case An internal successful run with no customer outcome leaves the post-delivery gap unresolved.
Failure and recovery Missing outcome evidence prevents closure; failed or rejected improvements remain visible without altering accepted guidance.
Acceptance bookkeeper.outcome.submit@0.1.0 and close@0.1.0 keep separate evidence and named review before any promotion.
Evidence references docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Three-Gap contract; docs/BOOKKEEPER_OUTCOME_CLOSURE.md
Portable agent pack inspect verify merge upgrade rollback
MOONBOOK-UC06 | Baseline scope. Move and evolve a portable agent pack.
1. Pack and inspect the local book and declared capabilities.
2. Verify a proposed merge or upgrade against protected accepted evidence.
3. Apply a reviewed change and retain a rollback path to the preceding version.
Edge case A valid local pack is not cross-machine deployment qualification.
Failure and recovery A conflict, incompatible version or failed verification leaves the installed accepted pack unchanged and supports rollback.
Acceptance A fixture upgrade and rollback preserve accepted evidence and expose rejected changes; cross-machine behavior requires separate qualification.
Evidence references docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Capability status; docs/PORTABLE_EVOLVING_AGENT.md
Company source ledger
MOONBOOK-F01 | Roadmap scope. Proposed extension after the owning baseline workflow and authority gates are qualified.
1. Assign each company source a stable origin and snapshot identity.
2. Record retrieval time, excerpt, counterevidence and independently defined review state.
3. Expose a decision-oriented ledger that follows links back to raw input.
Edge case A trustworthy import of a false claim must remain factually unreviewed.
Failure and recovery When origin disappears preserve the lawful snapshot and mark current accessibility separately.
Acceptance A founder can distinguish source availability, byte integrity and claim acceptance without ambiguous labels.
Evidence references Proposed from product gaps and OPC OS handoff assessment
Outcome linked memory
MOONBOOK-F02 | Roadmap scope. Proposed extension after the owning baseline workflow and authority gates are qualified.
1. Receive exact commitment, task, artifact and outcome references from MoonProj.
2. Bind these to one Bookkeeper outcome record with unresolved gaps visible.
3. Retrieve the record when a later decision shares the relevant conditions.
Edge case Matching topic alone is insufficient to reuse a customer-specific lesson outside its scope.
Failure and recovery Missing or conflicting receipts prevent automatic closure and ask for review.
Acceptance A later decision exposes the reviewed lesson and its applicability without altering the original outcome evidence.
Evidence references Proposed from product gaps and OPC OS handoff assessment
Founder correction review
MOONBOOK-F03 | Roadmap scope. Proposed extension after the owning baseline workflow and authority gates are qualified.
1. Present differences between intended, executed and observed results.
2. Let the founder accept, reject or edit the proposed reusable rule.
3. Record reviewer, scope and version before making the rule available to consumers.
Edge case A correction may supersede guidance without deleting evidence that justified the earlier decision.
Failure and recovery Rejected changes leave accepted guidance untouched and retain the reasoning summary.
Acceptance A version change can be traced to named review and can be rolled back without corrupting raw sources.
Evidence references Proposed from product gaps and OPC OS handoff assessment

Data state and authority
Raw source and provenance
Owner MoonBook raw evidence store
State model Imported snapshot, derived page, superseded snapshot; source review semantics pending decision
Write authority Import permission does not prove factual authority; reviewer must be explicitly named for claim acceptance
Retention and recovery Retain original bytes, digest and origin; preserve lawful evidence under a documented retention policy
Accepted knowledge and review history
Owner MoonBook curator or named reviewer
State model Proposed claim, in review, accepted, rejected, corrected or superseded as proposed explicit semantics
Write authority Human review promotes reusable knowledge; external runtime cannot self-approve
Retention and recovery Preserve prior versions and review events; never repair old timestamps by guessing
Generated projections and executable declarations
Owner MoonBook generator with owning runtime for execution
State model Generated, exported, imported for review, activated under separate authority
Write authority Projection generation cannot accept knowledge or grant a new capability
Retention and recovery Rebuild projections from source; preserve exact version and digest for execution links

Delivery roadmap
Phase 1 Qualify the baseline
Reproduce the product's critical local, contract and configured paths with exact versions and observable receipts. Close missing prerequisites without widening authority.
Requirements MOONBOOK-B01, MOONBOOK-B02, MOONBOOK-B03, MOONBOOK-B04, MOONBOOK-B05, MOONBOOK-B06
Exit gate Every P0 baseline acceptance has an explicit result and evidence locator; unresolved dependency and integration states remain visible.
Phase 2 Complete one connected outcome
Implement the smallest useful cross-product scenario and test review, interruption and recovery with real authorized inputs.
Requirements MOONBOOK-R01, MOONBOOK-R02
Exit gate A named user accepts the exact output and can recover its evidence chain without reconstructing identities manually.
Phase 3 Expand only after measured value
Add remaining proposed workflows where observed use demonstrates reduced coordination cost or a distinct required capability.
Requirements MOONBOOK-R03
Exit gate Measure the outcome against the simpler alternative, include review overhead, and retain the ability to stop or narrow low-value scope.

Non goals

Alternatives and optimality
Plain Markdown with version control
Advantage Simple, portable and directly editable evidence notes
Cost Review queues, outcome linkage and trusted projections need conventions or scripts
When to choose Use for a small static knowledge base where execution and multi-book review add little value
A conventional hosted wiki
Advantage Familiar collaborative review and access controls
Cost Executable intent and exact evidence lineage may require integrations
When to choose Choose if collaboration and managed backup are more urgent than local programmable books
Reusable reviewed memory is a strong OPC boundary, but the PRD is not optimal while accepted and verified can mean both successful import and factual review. Resolve terminology and migration behavior before adding more automated learning.
A narrow improvement can preserve existing workflows by splitting integrity status from claim-review status and adding an explicit source-review transition. Merely changing every imported source to pending would be incomplete if no approval route exists. This is a proposed design requiring the founder's choice.
Prioritize a complete correction lifecycle and one retrievable outcome lesson. Cross-machine portability and multi-user hosting should advance only with backup, restore and compatibility acceptance, rather than being implied by the breadth of the CLI.
Open product decisions
Decision What should accepted and verified mean for an imported source?
Recommendation Keep import integrity and human claim review as separate fields and provide a source-review transition.
Consequence The current labels cannot be used as evidence of factual approval; default changes need compatible migration.
Decision What qualifies an outcome for closure?
Recommendation Require observable outcome evidence and a named decision on the proposed correction.
Consequence Internal execution alone leaves customer and commercial learning incomplete.
