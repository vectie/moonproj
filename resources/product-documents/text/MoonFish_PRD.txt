MoonFish product requirements
OPC OS portfolio  |  30 September 2026  |  Version 1 for product review
MoonFish is a governed, shadow-only financial research pack for A-shares and exchangeable bonds. Its frozen baseline produces deterministic research, independent challenge, named paper release and attributable paper outcomes. It must never submit a trade or change company cash. Local follow-up tests passed 8/8; licensed point-in-time data, permitted commercial use and the required 120 forward shadow sessions remain unverified. These are release gates, not evidence that the product has failed them.
Repository maturity claim shadow-only alpha
This document defines the product baseline and a separately proposed roadmap. Acceptance tests below are release criteria unless a cited observation explicitly says they passed. Current source coverage does not establish production deployment or customer value.
Product philosophy and scope
A useful research product makes the chain from market observation to paper decision inspectable. Price policy, data cutoff, factor evidence, contradictions and invalidation belong beside the conclusion. A model may explain or challenge a deterministic result but may not quietly change scores, weights, exits or the governing 40–65-session policy. A 10× outcome is an extreme bull scenario, never an expected return or default sales claim.
For a one-person company, this pack belongs only in a legitimate financial research offer or a separately governed research activity. A founder can commit to producing an inspected research packet and learn from paper results; that commitment is distinct from investment execution, client acceptance and cash receipt. MoonProj remains responsible for business accounting. The first product should be a repeatable shadow research service rather than a broad autonomous investing workspace.

User scenarios
A share candidate dossier and next session plan
MF-W01 | Baseline scope. A researcher requests the next session screen
1. Bind the licensed point-in-time bundle, cutoff and exact policy revision
2. Compute a deterministic set of zero to five candidates
3. Inspect factor evidence, contradictions, entry context, goal and invalidation
4. Save a next-session paper plan for review
Edge case Zero eligible candidates is a complete result; missing observations cannot be replaced with current hindsight data.
Failure and recovery A stale bundle or undefined policy boundary blocks release and preserves an incomplete dossier for correction.
Acceptance Replaying identical inputs returns identical candidates; a plan outside the declared 40–65-session policy is rejected.
Evidence references docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#User-visible recommendation; README.mbt.md#Product Scope And Boundary
Independent challenge and named release
MF-W02 | Baseline scope. A scored dossier awaits review
1. Freeze scores and source references
2. Collect separately attributed bull and bear challenges
3. Record reviewer disposition of unresolved contradictions
4. Release only the exact reviewed paper revision
Edge case A persuasive narrative cannot override a deterministic exit; disagreement can remain visible in a withheld recommendation.
Failure and recovery Missing reviewer identity or changed evidence invalidates release and returns the packet to review.
Acceptance The release records who reviewed which revision; altered weights require a new policy revision, not a prose edit.
Evidence references docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Data and recommendation policy
Paper account replay backtest and attribution
MF-W03 | Baseline scope. A released plan produces a hypothetical fill or exit
1. Record paper assumptions and costs
2. Append immutable account and NAV revisions
3. Replay or backtest with explicit historical mode
4. Attribute realized after-cost outcomes to the original policy
5. Accrue eligible forward shadow sessions separately
Edge case A backtest with 120 observations is not 120 forward sessions; a no-position session remains identifiable.
Failure and recovery A duplicate event returns its existing revision; a broken parent chain stops account advancement.
Acceptance Recompute NAV and attribution from stored events; account labels remain paper throughout export and restart.
Evidence references docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Capability status; README.mbt.md#Current Product Surfaces
Exchangeable bond daily close screen
MF-W04 | Baseline scope. An operator requests the EB closing table
1. Select the independent EB policy and close date
2. Apply inclusive eligibility thresholds
3. Produce the seven-column close table
4. Compare the six-stage lifecycle against the previous close
Edge case An instrument exactly at a threshold is treated by the inclusive rule; missing lifecycle evidence stays unknown.
Failure and recovery An A-share classifier or policy cannot silently handle an EB; incomplete close data is flagged before release.
Acceptance Boundary fixtures verify inclusive eligibility and stage deltas without conflating EB and A-share results.

Price action migration reference
MF-W05 | Baseline scope. A maintainer evaluates a replacement analysis path
1. Retain the original market snapshot and deterministic indicators
2. Compare routing and validation outputs
3. Run the documented two-stage analysis
4. Record cutover evidence and a reversible rollback point
Edge case The migration foundation is a reference path, not an extra promise of live strategy coverage.
Failure and recovery A material output difference blocks cutover until explained; failed migration restores the prior version without rewriting its outcomes.
Acceptance A reviewer can reconstruct old and new outputs from the same snapshot and recover the previous route.

Edge case An apparently attractive candidate may remain withheld because commercial data use is not demonstrated.
Failure and recovery Absent gate evidence yields an actionable blocker instead of a publish-ready badge.

Paper outcome to reviewed learning
MF-W07 | Roadmap scope. A paper position closes or a thesis expires
1. Bind the after-cost result to its policy and released dossier
2. Draft a lesson separating data, decision and hindsight
3. Request named review and durable MoonBook receipt
Edge case A rejected lesson remains in the research history; it does not become approved company knowledge.
Failure and recovery An unavailable knowledge host leaves a retryable export, never a false persistence confirmation.
Acceptance The reviewed outcome carries exact paper identity and cannot populate a MoonProj cash balance.

Alternatives and optimality
Licensed terminal and research notebook
Advantage Fast for one analyst and few decisions
Cost Manual provenance and inconsistent replay
When to choose Prefer when a single researcher can maintain a trustworthy review trail without integration
Spreadsheet paper portfolio
Advantage Transparent calculations with low setup cost
Cost Revision and temporal integrity need discipline
When to choose Prefer for a small fixed strategy; adopt MoonFish when reproducibility and review coordination justify it
Broad autonomous investing platform
Advantage Could unite research and execution
Cost Large authority, regulatory and safety burden
When to choose Exclude from this PRD; shadow research is the narrower defendable boundary
The strongest early product is a small, inspectable paper recommendation packet. Zero candidates must be a legitimate outcome, reducing pressure to manufacture opportunities for a dashboard. Reviewers should concentrate on whether evidence supports a decision.
The separation of deterministic policy and narrative challenge is defensible. Practical value remains conditional on licensed point-in-time inputs and forward results. Local tests cannot establish data rights, commercial viability, suitability or future returns.
A narrower first release could offer one A-share policy, a separately labeled EB report and manual reviewed learning export. A notebook is better when volume and audit burden are small. Expansion should follow measured reviewer effort, rejected releases, shadow attribution and buyer acceptance. This is a constrained product hypothesis, not a globally optimal investment or founder operating system.
