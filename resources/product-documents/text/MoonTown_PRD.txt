MoonTown product requirements
OPC OS portfolio  |  30 September 2026  |  Version 1 for product review
MoonTown should coordinate recurring goals and cross-book evidence for operators who already maintain several useful books. Its adapter and lifecycle tests support bounded local contracts. The next product question is whether recurring synthesis saves more founder attention than supervision costs; autonomous company operation is outside the product claim.
Repository maturity claim experimental local alpha
This document defines the product baseline and a separately proposed roadmap. Acceptance tests below are release criteria unless a cited observation explicitly says they passed. Current source coverage does not establish production deployment or customer value.
Product philosophy and scope
A standing goal needs a durable purpose, source boundary, acceptance criterion and named reviewer. Town membership, scheduling and synthesis can provide leverage, but MoonBook continues to own accepted knowledge and the founder continues to own company decisions. The mayor and resident metaphors should not obscure who can approve an action.
Introduce MoonTown after a basic commitment and book workflow already works. Its output should be a small number of meaningful review items and contradictions. A busy spatial view or many worker messages cannot count as useful coordination. Measure decisions improved and human attention consumed.
Users and jobs
multi book operator
Supervise standing goals across multiple books with recoverable scheduling.
Trigger Several recurring evidence lanes create material manual follow-up.
Successful outcome The operator sees due work, active runs and review queues without duplicate dispatch.
founder with recurring research lanes
Receive meaningful changes to customer or market assumptions within an attention budget.
Trigger A recurring company decision depends on outside signals.
Successful outcome The founder gets a small sourced decision queue whose value exceeds review overhead.
book Keeper
Maintain the participating book and review proposed cross-book updates.
Trigger A goal requests book context or a synthesis proposes a knowledge change.
Successful outcome Book truth changes only through the book-owned acceptance transition.
Mayor supervisor role
Coordinate roles and intervene in blocked or conflicting work.
Trigger The town has a stalled goal, source conflict or supervision exception.
Successful outcome The supervisor can reconcile or pause work without inheriting company or physical authority.

User scenarios
Town bootstrap  book catalog  standing goals and supervised daemon
MOONTOWN-UC01 | Baseline scope. Bootstrap and supervise a town with standing goals.
1. Create durable town, book catalog and standing-goal records.
2. Plan due work and run the supported one-shot, foreground or supervised background mode.
3. Inspect health, worker lifecycle and restart state without losing goal intent.
Edge case Two supervisors must not independently dispatch the same due goal with conflicting state.
Failure and recovery After a daemon interruption reconcile existing tasks and due state before another dispatch; preserve failures and restart evidence.
Acceptance A restart fixture resumes the same goal ledger and exposes unresolved work instead of presenting an empty healthy town.
Evidence references README.mbt.md#Current Status; docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Capability status
MoonBook Keeper lanes and MoonClaw proposal run lifecycle
MOONTOWN-UC02 | Baseline scope. Dispatch a Keeper lane through participating runtimes.
1. Select a reviewed participating book and a bounded standing goal.
2. Create the MoonClaw proposal/run through the configured adapter and poll its state.
3. Persist the result and surface it for the proper book review.
Edge case A decoded book state is not proof that the gateway, model route or authority is ready.
Failure and recovery Missing runtime, stale state or failed polling leaves the task blocked and retains its known run identity.
Acceptance Qualify one configured goal-to-run-to-review path; current lifecycle and adapter tests are component evidence only.
Evidence references README.mbt.md#Current Status; docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Integration contract
Cross book synthesis and research salon
MOONTOWN-UC03 | Baseline scope. Synthesize across books in a research salon.
1. Select a scoped question and explicit book inputs.
2. Use the versioned civic synthesis operation to compare findings and contradictions.
3. Deliver a review-pending synthesis to the named human or book curator.
Edge case The research-salon name is scenario configuration, not a callable operation ID or authority grant.
Failure and recovery Unavailable books or incompatible snapshots remain named gaps and cannot be replaced with invented consensus.
Acceptance A synthesis cites its input snapshots and remains pending review until the owning book accepts a change.
Evidence references README.mbt.md#What MoonTown Feels Like; README.mbt.md#Final Integration Portfolio
Planbook cookbook  PDF evidence watch and book template requests
MOONTOWN-UC04 | Baseline scope. Recover planned work through Planbook and Cookbook.
1. Write intent and acceptance conditions in plan.md before dispatch.
2. Select a documented recipe such as PDF evidence watch or a book-template request.
3. Recover the plan and decision context after interruption or a book request transition.
Edge case A recipe description does not prove that every product has a universal plan-first user experience.
Failure and recovery A missing or invalid plan blocks execution or prompts repair while retaining existing evidence and task identity.
Acceptance A recovery exercise reconstructs why the work exists and how it will be accepted from the plan, without private runtime state.
Evidence references README.mbt.md#Planbook Workflow; docs/PLANBOOK.md#plan.md Contract; docs/COOKBOOK.md
Civic communication and spatial operator view
MOONTOWN-UC05 | Baseline scope. Use civic communication and the spatial operator view.
1. Inspect membership, roles and current communication in the town view.
2. Drill into the exact goal, book or review item behind an activity.
3. Let the supervisor intervene within the owning service's authority boundary.
Edge case Movement, role activity or message counts must not be labeled business outcomes.
Failure and recovery Stale frontend/backend synchronization is visible and disables misleading state-changing controls.
Acceptance Every actionable visual item resolves to a durable record and displays the freshness of its state.
Evidence references README.mbt.md#Current Capabilities; docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md#Ownership
Recurring company signal watch
MOONTOWN-F01 | Roadmap scope. Proposed extension after the owning baseline workflow and authority gates are qualified.
1. Create one source-bounded watch tied to a company assumption.
2. Compare new evidence with the previous reviewed state and suppress immaterial repeats.
3. Send a meaningful change with provenance to the MoonProj decision queue.
Edge case Frequent source noise should not increase founder notifications without changed decision relevance.
Failure and recovery Unavailable sources produce a bounded health item rather than fabricated absence of change.
Acceptance One changed assumption produces one review item with the exact source and a clear next owner action.
Evidence references Proposed from product gaps and OPC OS handoff assessment
Cross book contradiction review
MOONTOWN-F02 | Roadmap scope. Proposed extension after the owning baseline workflow and authority gates are qualified.
1. Compare related claims across explicitly selected books.
2. Produce one contradiction record with both sources and applicable contexts.
3. Let a reviewer resolve, preserve or defer the conflict without silently editing either book.
Edge case Two claims can both be valid under different dates or conditions.
Failure and recovery A source update invalidates the old comparison and requests re-review rather than reusing the earlier verdict.
Acceptance Both book owners can trace the same reversible review item and see whether accepted knowledge changed.

Coordination budget
MOONTOWN-F03 | Roadmap scope. Proposed extension after the owning baseline workflow and authority gates are qualified.
1. Set an attention and execution budget per standing goal.
2. Measure useful decisions, manual follow-up avoided and review overhead.
3. Pause or simplify low-value coordination with owner approval.
Edge case A reduction in worker runtime can still increase human review burden.

Alternatives and optimality
One book and a recurring checklist
Advantage Minimal setup and clear human ownership
Cost Cross-book contradictions and repetitive polling remain manual
When to choose Prefer until several independent recurring evidence lanes create material follow-up cost
A basic scheduled query with a review inbox
Advantage Simple recurring signal detection with less metaphor and role machinery
Cost Limited durable multi-book coordination and recovery lineage
When to choose Use when one watch and one reviewer suffice; earn additional Town complexity through measured benefit
MoonTown has a defensible role as an advanced coordination layer. It is not the optimal beginner front door without evidence that the extra roles and spatial UI reduce attention cost. Start with one recurring source-bound watch and one review queue.
The product needs multi-day recovery, authentication and backup qualification before standing goals become trusted infrastructure. Passing state decoding is valuable integration evidence but does not prove continuous service operation.
The strongest roadmap addition is an explicit coordination budget. It makes the simpler alternative a first-class decision and prevents worker activity from becoming a self-justifying metric.
Open product decisions
Decision Which recurring decision is costly enough to justify Town?
Recommendation Choose one repeated assumption check with a named reviewer and measurable manual baseline.
Consequence Without that comparison, more synthesis may simply create more review work.
Decision When may synthesis alter a book?
Recommendation Only through the owning book's explicit review and acceptance transition.
Consequence Town coordination receipts alone must never become accepted knowledge.
