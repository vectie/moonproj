MoonProj product requirements
OPC OS portfolio  |  30 September 2026  |  Version 1 for product review
MoonProj should be the accountable founder's commitment and company-state spine. The immediate product priority is one buyer problem carried into authorized work and independently evidenced delivery and cash. The browser-local work packet is useful today; the signed live company path and commercial closure are release gates, not demonstrated outcomes.
Repository maturity claim migration/operator-preview alpha
Product philosophy and scope
The company owns obligations, resources and business facts. A person authorizes material commitments, while agents can produce bounded proposals and artifacts. The primary screen should answer what was promised, to whom, what evidence supports it, who must decide next and what remains unpaid. A broad ERP capability inventory is a supporting domain model, not the starting navigation for a one-person company.
Freeze the baseline around truthful drafting, signed-command semantics and domain boundaries. Keep the synthetic commercial kernel as a contract test. The connected timeline, external customer response and cash reconciliation are a separate roadmap. Expanding desired scope must not erase earlier local success or convert test success into revenue evidence.
Users and jobs
one person company founder owner
Choose a buyer problem and hold one commitment from source through cash evidence.
Trigger A buyer request, market signal or unresolved promise needs a next action.
Successful outcome The owner can distinguish draft, authorized work, customer response and actual collection.
company operator
Prepare and reconcile company tasks without broadening their authority.
Trigger An approved work packet needs canonical creation, progress reporting or exception handling.
Successful outcome The task has the correct actor, idempotency identity and event receipt, or an explicit blocker.
named financial or business approver
Review the exact budget, procurement, accounting or treasury action within the assigned authority.
Trigger A domain transition needs approval or a shadow ERP result differs from the authoritative system.
Successful outcome The approver can accept, reject or request correction with the original evidence retained.
bounded agent as proposal producer
Produce a bounded plan, research result or artifact against the stated acceptance criterion.
Trigger A person authorizes an exact proposal or runtime task.
Successful outcome The agent returns inspectable output and receipts without signing, paying or accepting its own work.

Alternatives and optimality
A document and spreadsheet with an existing invoicing tool
Advantage Lowest adoption and integration cost for one offer and one buyer
Cost Manual provenance, authority and retry history can fragment
When to choose Use while validating the first paid workflow if software coordination would exceed the work itself
Existing ERP with a narrow founder dashboard
Advantage Keeps a proven financial system of record and established controls
Cost Cross-product research and artifact links may remain manual
When to choose Prefer during shadow migration; add MoonProj authority only after reconciliation passes
The proposed PRD is conditionally stronger when it centers one commitment and keeps financial invariants behind progressive disclosure. Its value is not the number of ERP modules. The first release should make an observed buyer problem, one authorized task and the exact delivered artifact easier to track than a shared document.
The largest product risk is completing internal work without learning whether a buyer accepted or paid. A signed live task rehearsal is necessary but insufficient. Qualify demand and external closure before widening automation or domain scope.
Baseline completion should be reported per bounded path. Local draft continuity can be fulfilled while signed service availability remains blocked and the broader commercial path remains proposed. A single aggregate maturity percentage would hide these materially different conclusions.
Open product decisions

Recommendation Choose one design partner and one artifact acceptance test before expanding the dashboard.
Consequence Without a buyer commitment, delivery tests demonstrate capability but cannot establish product value.
Decision What system owns financial truth during migration?
Recommendation Keep the existing ERP authoritative until module-by-module reconciliation and explicit cutover.
Consequence Premature dual authority risks conflicting obligations, receipts and reporting.
